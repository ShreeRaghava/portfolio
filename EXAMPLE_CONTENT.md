## 🎨 WRITING TIPS

### Good Practice:
✅ Clear and concise language  
✅ Action-oriented descriptions  
✅ Specific results and metrics  
✅ Professional but friendly tone  
✅ Focus on benefits and impact  

### Avoid:
❌ Too technical jargon  
❌ Long paragraphs  
❌ Vague descriptions  
❌ Overused clichés  
❌ Negative language
